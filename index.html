Resistance was futile

Every officer on the deck has been added to the Collective.

Click / tap the deck to place the selected unit. Drones hunt on their own — you command the Collective by placement, not by orders. Officers shoot back — and the Borg adapt to any frequency fired at them too often. Bulkheads block everything — movement and phaser fire. Drones path through doorways; officers flee room to room.

How it works

  • Drones are basically zombies. They shamble toward the nearest officer, always. Slow, stupid, inevitable.
  • Contact starts the process. A drone in arm's reach injects its tubules and drains the officer's Resistance — watch the ring around the officer empty.
  • Officers run. They're a touch slower than a drone, but if one breaks contact its Resistance slowly recovers. Corner them.
  • Swarm to speed it up. Every extra drone in contact drains Resistance faster. Surround your target.
  • The Collective adapts. Each assimilation makes every drone a little faster. It does not go back down.
  • Resistance hits zero → drone. The officer joins the Collective and immediately starts hunting its former crewmates.
  • Every class carries a different phaser. Command (red) hauls a Type III rifle — heavy hits, slow fire. Operations (gold) carries the balanced Type II. Science (teal) gets the Type I — weak, but it fires fast.
  • Drones can die now. Phaser fire burns through a drone's hull — a health bar appears over damaged drones. Lose every drone and the deck belongs to Starfleet.
  • Borg shields adapt to frequency. Each officer's phaser sits on one of four frequencies (the coloured dot). Just 3 hits on the same frequency and the Collective adapts: a bright green shield bubble appears with an ADAPTED tag, and that frequency is neutralised completely — shots just splash off. Watch the four dots in the status panel fill toward adaptation.
  • Officers remodulate. Once their frequency is adapted to, an officer needs about 6 seconds to find a new one — that's your window to close in.
  • Only Captains get the adaptive phaser. Four pips and a gold ring. It changes frequency with every single shot, so the Borg can never adapt to it — a Captain is the most dangerous officer on the deck.
  • This is a starship now. The Borg beam in through the Transporter Room (top left). The deck is six rooms — Bridge, Science Lab, Cargo Bay, Engineering with its glowing warp core, and Sickbay — linked by a central corridor and side doorways.
  • Bulkheads are real walls. Nothing walks or shoots through them — phaser beams stop at the wall, so an officer behind a bulkhead is safe until a drone comes through a door. Drones navigate doorway to doorway on their own.
  • Rooms change the hunt. Officers flee through doors into other rooms, and a drone swarm has to funnel through the doorway to follow — but corner a crew member in a room and there are only two doors out.
